# Membership terms

Version {{ MS.terms_version }}, in force from 1 January 2027. These are the terms you accept when you sign up online. We also email them to you when you sign up. The Spanish version prevails if the two ever differ.

## 1. Who we are

{{ FT.org }}, a non-profit association with tax number (CIF) {{ FT.cif }}, registered at {{ FT.address | replace: '<br>', ', ' }}, Spain. Email: [info@aikidomusubi.com](mailto:info@aikidomusubi.com). Phone and WhatsApp: +34 717 171 177.

## 2. What the fee covers, and what it does not

The **general fee** gives access to every class the association runs, at every space. The **sports-centre rate** gives access only to the classes at the sports centre whose membership you hold.

The fee does not include the federation licence and insurance (section 13), dan gradings, seminars and courses, or a keikogi, except the one we give with quarterly and annual plans (section 14). Kyū gradings are free.

## 3. Fees, the family discount and the sports-centre rate

The amount of each fee, by age group and period, is the one published on [Fees]({{ MS.urls.fees.en }}) on the day you sign up, which forms part of these terms. There are two age groups: up to 16, and 17 and over.

**Family discount.** One membership may include several people from the same household, with a single account holder who pays and one billing period for everyone. The first person pays the full fee and each one after that pays {{ fam }} € less a month ({{ fam_q }} € less a quarter, {{ fam_y }} € less a year).

**Sports-centre rate.** If you hold a membership of the sports centre at one of our municipal spaces, you may pay that space's reduced fee, which covers its classes only, is paid monthly and does not combine with the family discount: a person on it neither receives nor gives rise to the discount. Show your membership card at the first class. If it is not valid, you move to the general fee from the next charge and we tell you by email.

## 4. Billing, on the 5th

The fee is charged on the 5th of each month, quarter or year, depending on the period you choose. When you sign up you pay the share for the days up to the next 5th; from that day, the full fee. If you sign up with an invitation from the dojo, your first charge may be directly on the 5th it gives.

You pay exactly the published amount: there is no surcharge for the payment method.

## 5. Payment methods

You pay by card or SEPA direct debit on a secure Stripe page; Stripe processes payments on the association's behalf. The association neither sees nor stores your card or account details. With direct debit, Stripe notifies you before each debit.

If a charge fails, we try again over the following days and let you know. Your membership remains active while we sort it out with you. If it remained unpaid after we had told you, we could end it.

## 6. Duration and renewal

The membership has no end date: it renews automatically at the end of each period and is charged on the 5th. **Fifteen days before every renewal we email you** with the date, the amount and how to cancel if you do not wish to continue.

## 7. Cancelling

You can cancel whenever you like, with no penalty, in your [members area]({{ MS.urls.members.en }}), by email, by phone or at the dojo. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid: until then you can keep training, and nothing more is charged. We confirm it by email.

## 8. Refunds

The unused part of a paid period, monthly, quarterly or annual, is not refunded, except when you exercise the right of withdrawal (section 12).

## 9. Changing people or period

To add or remove people, or change the billing period, write to us. The change applies from the next charge, with the family discount recalculated, and we confirm it by email with the amounts.

## 10. Moving to the 17-and-over fee

For anyone in the up-to-16 group we keep only the month and year of birth. They move to the 17-and-over fee from the first charge in a month after the month of their 17th birthday. We email you when it happens, and at that moment we delete their date of birth.

## 11. Price changes

If the general assembly approves new fees, we tell you in writing **at least one month in advance**. If you do not agree, you can cancel before they apply, at no cost.

## 12. Right of withdrawal

You may withdraw from the membership within **14 calendar days** of the day you sign up, without giving a reason. The simplest way is the [withdrawal form]({{ MS.urls.withdrawal.en }}), which sends you an acknowledgement with the date and time; any clear statement by email, phone or at the dojo also counts.

When you sign up you expressly ask to start training straight away, before those 14 days are over. So if you withdraw, you pay only the share for the days up to when you tell us, and we refund the rest **within 14 days**, by the same means of payment.

**Model withdrawal form.** To {{ FT.org }}, {{ FT.address | replace: '<br>', ', ' }}, Spain, info@aikidomusubi.com: I hereby give notice that I withdraw from my contract for a training membership concluded on [date]. Name, address, signature (only if sent on paper) and date.

## 13. Federation licence and insurance

Sports insurance is compulsory for training, and it comes with the federation licence. It costs 45 € a year per person, is paid in March, separately from the fee, and the dojo arranges it through Arashi Group. The licence carries the right to be examined.

## 14. The free keikogi

On a quarterly or annual plan, each person receives a keikogi on their first enrolment. We hand it over at the dojo once the first full quarter or year has been paid and the 14 days to withdraw are over. It is one per person: a change of period or a new enrolment does not include another, and it cannot be exchanged for money.

## 15. Minors

The account holder must be an adult. If the membership includes anyone under 18, the account holder declares that they are that person's parent or legal guardian. The paper enrolment form, signed by whoever holds parental authority or guardianship, is still required.

## 16. Data protection

How we handle your data is set out in the [privacy policy]({{ MS.urls.privacy.en }}).

## 17. Complaints and contact

For any question or complaint, write to [info@aikidomusubi.com](mailto:info@aikidomusubi.com) or call +34 717 171 177. Official complaint forms are available at the dojo.

## 18. Governing law

These terms are governed by Spanish law. If you are a consumer, you may go to the courts of your place of residence.
