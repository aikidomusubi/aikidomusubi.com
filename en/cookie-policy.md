# Cookie Policy

Which cookies this website uses, what they do, and how to accept or reject them.

**Last updated: 13 August 2026**

> This English version is provided for convenience. In the event of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails, as this website is operated under Spanish law.

This website, **aikidomusubi.com**, owned by Aikido Musubi, uses cookies and similar technologies. This Cookie Policy has been prepared in line with the *Guide on the use of cookies* of the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

<hr>

## 1. What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on the user's browser or device when they visit it. Cookies allow the website to remember information about the visit, which can make browsing easier and more useful. There are also other technologies that perform similar functions (for example, pixels or local storage), which this policy also refers to.

## 2. Types of cookies we use

### a) Technical or necessary cookies (no consent required)
These are essential for the operation of the website and for remembering your cookie-settings preferences. They do not collect information for advertising or tracking purposes.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `aikidomusubi_cookie_consent` | Stores your choice regarding the acceptance or rejection of cookies. | 12 months |

### b) Third-party analytics cookies (consent required)
These allow us to understand, in aggregate and statistical form, how users use the website, in order to improve it. They are loaded through **Google Tag Manager** (identifier **GTM-KZ62VP5**), which may activate **Google Analytics**.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|----------|---------|----------|
| `_ga` | Google | Distinguish users (Google Analytics). | 2 years |
| `_ga_<ID>` | Google | Maintain session state (Google Analytics 4). | 2 years |
| `_gid` | Google | Distinguish users. | 24 hours |
| `_gat` | Google | Throttle the request rate. | 1 minute |

> Note: the specific cookies depend on the final configuration of Google Tag Manager. Review and adjust this table according to the tags you actually activate.

### c) Third-party cookies from embedded content
If the website embeds content from external platforms (for example, **YouTube videos** or **Facebook / Instagram** elements), those platforms may install their own cookies when you interact with that content. We recommend consulting their respective policies:
- [Google/YouTube Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Cookie Policy](https://www.facebook.com/policies/cookies/)

## 3. Consent

When you first access the website, a **cookie notice** is displayed that allows you to **accept all** or **reject all** non-necessary cookies. Analytics and third-party cookies **are not activated until you give your consent**. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.

## 4. How can you manage or withdraw your consent?

You can allow, block or delete the cookies installed on your device through your browser settings:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-us/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge)

Please note that disabling technical cookies may affect the correct functioning of the website.

## 5. Updates to the Cookie Policy

The Association may modify this Cookie Policy in response to new legislative or regulatory requirements, or in order to adapt it to the AEPD's instructions. We recommend reviewing it each time you access the website.

For any questions about this Cookie Policy, you can write to **info@aikidomusubi.com**.
