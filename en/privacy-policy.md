# Privacy Policy

What personal data we process, for what purpose and legal basis, for how long, and how to exercise your rights.

Aikido Musubi is committed to protecting the privacy of the users of its website, its members, students and anyone whose personal data it processes. This Privacy Policy has been drafted in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR") and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018, of 5 December, on the Protection of Personal Data and the guarantee of digital rights (LOPDGDD).

## 2. What data do we process, for what purpose and on what legal basis?

We process personal data according to your relationship with the Association:

### a) Enquiries via the website, email, phone or WhatsApp
- **Data:** name, email, phone number and any data you provide in your communication.
- **Purpose:** to handle and respond to your enquiry or request for information.
- **Legal basis:** your consent and/or the taking of pre-contractual steps at your request (Art. 6(1)(a) and 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### b) Registration and management of members and students
- **Data:** full name, ID/passport number, date of birth, postal address, phone, email, grade and data relating to aikido practice, and where applicable federation licence data.
- **Purpose:** to manage registration and the relationship as a member or student, organise classes and courses, process sports licences and insurance, and comply with the Association's obligations.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the membership relationship or contract to which you are a party (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), compliance with legal obligations applicable to the Association (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) and, where applicable, your consent.

### c) Online signup and fee payment
- **Account holder's data:** full name, email, phone (optional), billing address and payment method (card or bank account). The address and the payment method are collected directly by Stripe on its secure page: the Association neither sees nor stores card or account numbers.
- **Data of the people who train:** full name, age group, the fee applied and, only for those aged up to 16, the month and year of birth.
- **Acceptance data:** the version of the terms accepted, and the date and time of the acceptance, of the request to start before the withdrawal period ends, and of the declarations of being an adult and a parent or guardian.
- **Technical data:** IP address and browser, in the logs of the service that receives the signup, for at most 30 days and only for the security of the service.
- **Purpose:** to handle the signup, collect the fee, send receipts, renewal notices and confirmations, handle cancellations and withdrawals, and apply the change of fee at 17.
- **Legal basis:** the membership contract to which you are a party (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); compliance with consumer and tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) for evidence of acceptance and keeping receipts; and the legitimate interest in the security of the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for the technical data.
- **Where it is kept:** at Stripe, which processes the payments. The month and year of birth are deleted as soon as the 17-and-over fee applies.

### d) Data of minors
For the registration of minors under 14 years of age, the **consent of the parents or legal guardians is required**. They must provide their own identification and contact details in addition to those of the minor. The Association will process the minor's data exclusively to manage their participation in the activities, and will not process more data than is strictly necessary.

### e) Images and videos
- **Data:** images and video recordings of members, students and attendees taken during classes, courses, events and demonstrations.
- **Purpose:** to promote the Association's activities on the website and on social networks (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube).
- **Legal basis:** your express consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), obtained through the corresponding **image-use authorisation clause**. In the case of minors, this consent must be granted by their parents or legal guardians. You may withdraw this consent at any time.

### f) Browsing and cookies
- **Data:** browsing data and device identifiers, processed through cookies and similar technologies (for example, Google Analytics via Google Tag Manager).
- **Purpose:** to analyse the use of the website and produce statistics.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, given through the cookie notice. See the [Cookie Policy](/en/cookie-policy/) for more information.

## 3. How long do we keep the data?

- **Enquiry** data will be kept for as long as necessary to handle it and, thereafter, for up to 1 year, unless you request its deletion sooner.
- **Member and student** data will be kept for as long as the membership relationship lasts and, once ended, for the periods legally required to address possible liabilities (generally up to 4 years for tax matters, and other periods as required by applicable law).
- **Online membership** data will be kept while you are a member and then for the legal periods (generally up to 4 years for tax matters). Withdrawal requests are kept as evidence for the same time. Technical data, for at most 30 days.
- **Images and videos** will be kept for as long as consent is not withdrawn and there is an interest in promoting the activities.

## 4. Who do we share your data with?

As a general rule, the Association does not transfer data to third parties, except where legally required. However, your data may be shared with:

- **Public administrations and official bodies**, where there is a legal obligation.
- **Sports federations and insurance entities**, for the processing of sports licences and insurance, where applicable.
- **Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd.** (Ireland), which processes fee payments on the Association's behalf as a data processor and, for its own purposes (fraud prevention, legal and know-your-customer obligations), as an independent controller.
- **Cloudflare, Inc.**, which hosts the service that receives signups and the withdrawal form, and **Sendinblue SAS (Brevo)**, in France, which sends the membership emails, both as data processors.
- **Service providers acting as data processors**, such as the web hosting provider (GitHub, Inc.) and the analytics tools provider (Google). These providers only process the data in accordance with our instructions.
- **Social networks** (Facebook/Meta, Instagram, YouTube/Google), in relation to the content published on those platforms.

## 5. International data transfers

Some of the providers we use (such as **GitHub, Inc.**, **Google LLC**, **Stripe, LLC** and **Cloudflare, Inc.**) are located in the United States. Such international transfers are covered by the **EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework** and/or the **Standard Contractual Clauses** approved by the European Commission, which provide adequate safeguards under the GDPR.

## 6. What are your rights?

You have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data.
- Request the **rectification** of inaccurate data.
- Request the **erasure** of your data ("right to be forgotten").
- Request the **restriction** of processing.
- **Object** to processing.
- Request the **portability** of your data.
- **Withdraw consent** given at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

If you have an online membership, you can correct your name, address, phone and payment method yourself in the [members area](/en/members/).

You may exercise these rights by writing to **info@aikidomusubi.com** or to the postal address indicated in section 1, enclosing a copy of your identity document or equivalent document that allows your identity to be verified.

Likewise, if you consider that the processing of your data does not comply with the regulations, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)**, C/ Jorge Juan, 6, 28001 Madrid — [www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

## 7. Security measures

The Association has adopted the technical and organisational measures necessary to ensure the security of personal data and prevent its alteration, loss, unauthorised processing or access, taking into account the state of the art, the nature of the stored data and the risks to which it is exposed.
