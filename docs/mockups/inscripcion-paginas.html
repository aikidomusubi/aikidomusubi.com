Members area, withdrawal, terms, and the rest of the site

There is no database and no password. Stripe holds the subscription, the payment method and the invoices, and its customer portal is the members area. Our /miembros/ page explains it and opens it; the member signs in with their email and a one-time code that Stripe sends. We store nothing and maintain nothing.

One button

"Ir a mi cuota" opens Stripe's no-code portal login link. Every email we send links here, never to the portal directly, because a portal session link expires in minutes.

What is not self-service

People, period and email changes go through the dojo in v1. The spec says so, and it is right: the family price has to be recalculated, and the portal's "switch plan" knows nothing about families.

Indexing

Public but noindex: it is useful to members and to nobody arriving from a search.

Why a form and not "email us"

EU Directive 2023/2673 makes an online withdrawal function mandatory for contracts concluded on a website, from 19 June 2026: a clearly labelled link, a short form, and a "confirm withdrawal" button. The consumer then receives an acknowledgement with the content, the date and the time.

What happens behind it

The Worker emails the acknowledgement and the admin. The dojo cancels the subscription and refunds within 14 days. The refund is the one task still worked out by hand; a small command can calculate it (tab 4).

After the 14 days

The form points to the members area, because what remains is an ordinary cancellation: no refund, effective at the end of the period.
