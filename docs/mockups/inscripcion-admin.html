Admin · what the dojo receives, and what it does by hand

In the spec, the admin side is the Stripe dashboard, the admin@ mailbox and a few terminal commands. There is no back office to build, because Stripe is the record. These are the three emails that arrive, and below them every task the spec leaves to a person.

Buttons in yellow are a proposal, not the spec. The spec has the dojo mark keikogi, sports-centre checks and age changes by editing Stripe metadata by hand, in a text field, one subscription at a time. The proposal is to put a button in the email for each. Each button opens a confirmation page on the Worker, signed and expiring, and the change is made only when that page's own button is pressed. That second press matters, because mail scanners open every link in an email. No login and no database, and nobody types keikogi_due=m1,m3 into a box.

Not in the spec, and the one I would insist on

The spec says to cancel each member's bank debit "only after their first Stripe charge succeeds". If the bank debit for February goes out before the Stripe charge on 5 February, that rule makes the member pay twice for February.

The safe rule is the other way round: a member leaves the bank remittance for the first month that Stripe charges. If that first Stripe charge then fails, the dojo chases it, as it would any failed payment.

moreA few days before each remittance, this list: who to leave out, and who is still on the bank. confirm On what day does the dojo send its remittance to the bank?
