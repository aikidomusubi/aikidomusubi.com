One flaw found and fixed here: Stripe's "upcoming renewal" event also fires 15 days before the first full charge. For a signup on the 20th that means a "your fee renews" email the next day, for a fee that has not started. Renewal notice

Trigger: invoice.upcoming, 15 days before every renewal, monthly included (decided).

Why: Ley 10/2025 (TRLGDCU art. 97.1 p): date, amount, that it will continue and be charged, and how to stop it. The subject line carries the date and amount, so the notice works without being opened.

Monthly members get twelve of these a year. That was the board's decision, and it is worth knowing.
