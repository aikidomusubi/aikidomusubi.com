{%- comment -%} THE MOSAIC: five photographs of one venue, the first large. Approved as proposal 1 in docs/mockups/venue-photos.html, with the room-and-practice photograph in the large tile and the site's own venue grade. Params: photos (a list of gallery photos; defaults to venue.gallery), venue, lang. A venue with several galleries (the dojo) passes each list. THE CROP IS CSS, NOT AN EDIT. Each fills its tile; `--x`/`--y` is the focal point and `--z` scales around it, so the empty floor of a large room falls outside the frame and the file stays whole. `sizes` IS THE WIDTH THE IMAGE IS DRAWN AT, not the width of the tile. Two things make it wider than the tile: the zoom, and `object-fit: cover`, which scales a landscape photograph in a square tile to the tile's HEIGHT, so a 3:2 photo in a 472px-high tile is drawn 708px wide. Both are applied below, from the photograph's own proportions in _data/imgdim.yml. Ignoring them fetched a file one or two sizes too small and the crops went soft. Tile sizes measured on the built page at 1440: the large tile 470x472, the wide one 470x312, the small ones 151x152. At 375 the column is ~311px: the large tile 311x240, the wide one 311x176, the two small ones 151x160. Real s with alt text, never CSS backgrounds: a background is invisible to Google Images. The fifth tile is display:none on a phone, and lazy, so a phone never downloads it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign L = include.lang -%} {%- assign photos = include.photos | default: include.venue.gallery -%}
