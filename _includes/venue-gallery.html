{%- comment -%} THE MOSAIC: five photographs of one venue, the first large. Approved as proposal 1 in docs/mockups/venue-photos.html, with the room-and-practice photograph in the large tile and the site's own venue grade. Params: venue (an entry of _data/venues.yml with `gallery`), lang. THE CROP IS CSS, NOT AN EDIT. Each fills its tile; `--x`/`--y` is the focal point and `--z` scales around it, so the empty floor of a large room falls outside the frame and the file stays whole. `sizes` IS THE TILE TIMES THE ZOOM. A tile drawn at 470px with a 1.4 zoom shows a 658px-wide piece of the image; a `sizes` that ignored the zoom would fetch one width too small and the crop would go soft. The tile widths are measured on the built page: the large and wide tiles are half the content column (≈470px from 992px up), the three small ones a sixth of it (≈155px); below 768px the first two are the full column and the next two half of it. Real s with alt text, never CSS backgrounds: a background is invisible to Google Images. The fifth tile is display:none on a phone, and lazy, so a phone never downloads it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign L = include.lang -%} {%- assign v = include.venue -%}
