{%- comment -%} iCalendar feed of the weekly timetable, generated from _data/schedule.yml. Included by the four schedule.ics pages, one per language. A real file at a stable URL rather than a script-built download, because that is what lets a device hand it straight to its own calendar app — and what lets the same URL be subscribed to over webcal://, so the calendar tracks changes to the YAML instead of holding a copy from the day it was imported. DTSTART needs a concrete first occurrence. Liquid has no date arithmetic worth the name, so the anchor is Monday 1 January 2024 — a real Monday — and each weekday is that date plus its index. With BYDAY carrying the actual repeat, the anchor only has to be a date whose weekday matches the first day listed; everything after it comes from the rule. The VTIMEZONE block is not decoration. Times here are local Madrid wall clock, so without it a client has to guess the offset and the whole schedule lands an hour out for half the year. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign S = site.data.schedule
