{%- comment -%} The grading programme, as a panel over the Resources page. WHY A PANEL AND NOT SIX PAGES. The five sheets plus the cumulative one are one document read one at a time, always reached from the same list. Six pages would be six URLs in the sitemap, six hreflang sets and six near-identical layouts; a panel is one. The deep link is `?r=kyu3`, which the script reads on load and writes on open, so a sheet can still be sent to somebody. FIVE SHEETS ARE RENDERED, NOT SIX. The syllabus is cumulative, so "the whole programme" IS the 1st kyū sheet — the only difference is that the colours are turned on and the legend appears. Rendering it twice would be ten kilobytes of duplicate markup on a page that already carries the other five. PRINT. `@media print` in styles/resources.less hides the site and prints the open sheet alone on one side of one sheet of A4. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign E = site.data.exams -%} {%- assign S = site.data['exams-sheets'] -%} {%- assign L = page.lang -%}
