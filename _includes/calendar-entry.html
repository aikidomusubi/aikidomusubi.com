{%- comment -%} One entry inside a month cell, plus the detail panel it opens. Called from two places with the same parameters, because the calendar draws from two sources: the _events/ collection (seminars, masterclasses, collaborations — things with a page and a poster) and _data/calendar.yml (gradings, closures, cancellations — dated facts with nothing to link to). Normalising them here means the two can never drift apart visually. THE PANEL IS RENDERED, NOT BUILT BY SCRIPT. It sits here `hidden`, and calendar.js only shows and places it. That keeps all four translations in Liquid where the rest of the copy is, and it means the detail is in the HTML a crawler reads rather than assembled from data-* attributes at runtime. It is NOT a hover-only tooltip. WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.13 requires content revealed on hover to be dismissible, hoverable and persistent, and a hover tooltip does not exist at all on the phone where a calendar actually gets read. So the entry is a disclosure: click or Enter opens it, Escape or a click outside closes it, and on a pointer device hover opens it too. WITHOUT JAVASCRIPT THE PANEL IS SIMPLY OPEN. There is no on the entry any more — the link is the panel's button and nowhere else — so hiding the panel by default would put that link out of reach when the script is blocked. The `.no-js` rules in calendar.less lay every panel out inline instead. The month grid gets very tall that way, which is the right trade for a fallback almost nobody sees: nothing becomes unreachable.
