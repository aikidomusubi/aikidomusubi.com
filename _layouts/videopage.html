{%- comment -%} ONE VIDEO, ON ITS OWN PAGE — a "watch page". WHY THIS EXISTS. Search Console reported four videos as "Video isn't on a watch page", and that was structural rather than a mistake: all six live on /prensa-y-tv/, and Google will treat at most one video per URL as that URL's main content. Five of the six could never be indexed as video however the markup was written. A page whose main content IS the video is the only answer, and it is also the honest one — each of these is a television appearance worth its own address. /prensa-y-tv/ stays exactly what it was, the showcase, and every card on it now links here. What changed there is its structured data: it used to emit a VideoObject per card, which was the page claiming to be six watch pages at once. It emits an ItemList of links now, and the VideoObject lives on the page the video is actually on. THE STUB CARRIES ONLY `video:` AND A LANGUAGE. Everything else — the four titles, the four descriptions, the date, the YouTube id, the thumbnail — is already in _videos/ and is read from there.
