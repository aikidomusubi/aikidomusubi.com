What _data/locations.yml holds is the part that cannot be derived — the lede, the questions and the labels. Move a class an hour and these pages move with it. WHY THESE PAGES ARE NOT DOORWAY PAGES, since that is the thing to get right and the reason to keep it that way: * every fact is specific to that town's rooms — address, hours, teacher, the way in, the floor plan * nothing is copied from /clases/ or /cuotas/; where the long version is wanted there is a link, which is also the internal linking these pages need in order to be crawled at all * the last question is per town and answers "how is this different from the others", which a doorway page cannot answer honestly * there are three, one per town where the association actually teaches.
